What it does, and how to use it

What Day Planner Does For You

Day Planner brings your to-do list, today's schedule, and your notes together on one page, in the classic day-planner layout — so you're not juggling separate apps to see what's due, what's next, and what was decided. Everything you enter is saved straight to your own Google account, so it's there the next time you open it, on any device.

One-Time Setup: Your Day Planner Folder

lock Your Privacy

Day Planner only ever writes inside one folder — the one you create for it below. It cannot change anything else in your Google Drive. If you turn on the optional Gmail hub, it can also read the emails you label with 🎯, and it archives or moves to Trash an email only when you click Archive or Delete on the Triage tab. It never sends email, never deletes anything permanently, and never removes your 🎯 labels. The one exception: when you paste a Google Docs, Sheets, Slides, or Drive link into a note, it briefly reads that file's title (nothing else) so it can show a readable name instead of a raw link.

How to set it up:
  1. In Google Drive, create a folder named Day Planner.
  2. Link that folder to the app when prompted (a one-time step).
  3. From then on, your daily notes and monthly records are kept there automatically — nothing to save manually.

The Tabs

Tasks

format_list_bulleted Priorities and Status

Every task gets a priority letter and number: A for urgent, B for important, C for everything else, then a number for order within that group. Type #a, #b, or #c in the input, or click the priority selector. Click a task's status box to mark it: Open arrow_forward Completed arrow_forward Forwarded arrow_forward Canceled arrow_forward Delegated.

sticky_note_2 Adding Details to a Task

Use a pipe | in the task text to attach details, e.g. Call vendor | discuss Q3 scope and renewal terms. The title becomes "Call vendor", and the details are saved in the Google Task's details. Open them any time with the sticky note icon (sticky_note_2).

move_item Moving a Task to Today

Open Master Tasks to see everything on your longer-term list. When you're ready to work on one, click Move to Today — it's added to today's task list with a priority already assigned, no retyping.

Notes

view_agenda Note Cards and Categories

Each day's notes are split into cards, one per topic. Tick the category boxes on a card (Work, Personal, Meeting, Decision, Project) to group it, then pick a Category Filter to show only those cards. Everything is saved to that month's notes Doc in your Day Planner folder.

tag Logging a Decision So You Can Find It Later

Start a note card heading with #index [Topic], e.g. #index [Budget] Approved the Q3 budget. It shows up in the Monthly Index tab, in date order — a running record of the calls you've made, without any extra filing.

history Version History

Need an earlier version of your daily notes? Click the Version History icon (history) in the Notes column header to see hourly snapshots, compare them, and restore one with a click.

mic Talk Instead of Type

Click the microphone icon on any note to dictate it instead of typing. Use Space to start and stop, Esc to close.

Tag Reference

Tags are short markers you type (or the app adds) that tell Day Planner what to do with a line.

Automatic Tasks

Some tasks are created for you, so you never retype them.

mail How the Gmail Hub Works

The hub checks your 🎯 labels every few minutes, once its schedule is installed. Each email is handled once, so leaving the label on is safe. Other 🎯 labels (Read, Review, Meeting, NCMMS-Daily) are recognized but don't do anything yet.

Turning it on: the hub is off by default. Its settings live in the Config tab of the Day Planner Hub Config Sheet in your Day Planner folder. Set GMAIL_ROUTER to TRUE, check the trial run, then set DRY_RUN to FALSE. While DRY_RUN is TRUE, the hub only reports what it would do. Set TRIAGE_BOARD and NCMMS_CORE_PILOT to TRUE to show the Triage and Meeting Prep tabs. The Meeting Registry tab lists your recurring meetings and their notes Docs.

Every hub feature stays off until you turn it on there. A new Sheet starts with every hub feature off. To keep your old settings instead, restore it from the Drive trash and reopen this page.

If something fails: the thread shows Error in Triage. Fix the cause and press Retry; most errors are also retried automatically.

Search, Sync & Fixes

search Finding Anything Fast

Press Ctrl+K (or click the search icon in the top bar) to search across all your tasks, appointments, and notes at once — no need to know which tab something is in.

sync Keeping Calendar & Tasks in Sync

Click the sync icon in the top bar any time to reconcile your tasks and Google Calendar appointments. This also happens automatically in the background every few minutes, so the two rarely drift apart.

warning When Something Doesn't Save

If a change can't reach Google (for example, you're offline), a Needs attention panel lists it. Click Retry once you're back online, or dismiss it with the close button.

dark_mode Light or Dark

Click the sun/moon icon in the top bar to switch between light and dark themes. Your choice is remembered on that device.

Put It on Your Desktop or Phone

Day Planner works in any browser, but you can also install it as its own app window — no browser tabs or address bar, just your planner, one click away from your taskbar, dock, or home screen.

desktop_windows Google Chrome (Desktop)

Click the menu icon (⋮) in the top-right corner → Save and share (or More tools) → Create shortcut.... Check "Open as window", then click Create. Pin the shortcut to your Taskbar or Dock for one-click access.

laptop_mac Microsoft Edge (Desktop)

Click the menu icon (…) in the top-right corner → Apps → Install this site as an app. Keep the name as Day Planner and click Install. Edge opens it in its own app window from then on.

smartphone Phone or Tablet (iOS & Android)

iPhone/iPad (Safari): Tap the Share button, scroll down, and tap Add to Home Screen.
Android (Chrome): Tap the menu icon (⋮) and choose Add to Home screen or Install app.
