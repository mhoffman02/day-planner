Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-04

Day Planner is a personal-use daily planning tool built and maintained by a single developer for their own use, currently in limited testing with a small set of invited test users.

What data this app accesses

Day Planner connects to your own Google account via Google Sign-In (OAuth) and reads and writes only your own data, using these Google services on your behalf:

  • Google Calendar — to show and manage your appointments.
  • Google Tasks — to show and manage your tasks.
  • Google Drive (app-created files only) — to store your daily notes as files this app creates. Day Planner cannot see, list, or modify any other files in your Drive.
  • Google Drive (read-only, limited) — only to look up the title of a Google Docs/Sheets/Slides/Drive link you paste into a note, so it can display a readable name instead of a raw URL.
  • Google Docs — only when you explicitly create a meeting agenda document from within the app.

What this app does not do

  • There is no backend server operated by the developer that stores your data. All data lives in your own Google account.
  • No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking.
  • A local, on-device cache (your browser's IndexedDB) is used only to make the app work offline; it never leaves your device.

Revoking access

You can revoke Day Planner's access to your Google account at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access.
